What's in our treats

Just three simple ingredients, baked by hand:

  • Pumpkin puree
  • Peanut butter
  • Oat flour

Dog-safe, always

All of our ingredients are dog-safe. Our treats never contain chocolate, xylitol, grapes, or raisins.

Every dog is different. If your dog has food allergies or sensitivities, please check with your veterinarian before sharing new treats.

Our Team

Students building something good

The Clifton Builders Program

The Clifton Builders Program at the University of Nebraska–Lincoln is a selective leadership and entrepreneurship program for students with high potential to build businesses, lead teams, and make a positive impact in their communities. Through hands-on projects, mentorship, and strengths-based coaching, students develop real-world leadership and business skills. The program focuses on turning ideas into action and preparing students to become successful entrepreneurs and leaders.

Our Builder Team Project

Santa Paws is our Builder Team Project: a real, student-run business that we designed, built, and launched ourselves. From baking the treats to crafting the charms and packaging every order, each step is our own, and all of our profits go to charity.

Our Mission To make affordable, handmade treats and gifts that bring joy to dog lovers and their pups, while giving back to our community with every sale.

Giving Back

Every purchase helps

When you treat your pup, you're helping others, too. All of our profits go to I've Got A Name, so every bag of treats and every leash charm makes a real difference right here in Nebraska.

Our charity partner

I've Got A Name

I've Got A Name is a Lincoln nonprofit that builds trusting relationships with women and girls in Nebraska who are vulnerable to, or victims of, sexual exploitation. Through street outreach, community awareness, and its Men Against Demand program, it works to end sex trafficking in our state.
